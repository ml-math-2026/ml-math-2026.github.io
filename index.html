Mathematical Foundations of Modern Machine Learning

4th - 5th July, 2026

Peking University, China

Register Now

Modern machine learning has developed rapidly in both methodology and scale, while its mathematical foundations remain incomplete. Understanding the structural principles governing expressivity, representation, generalization, optimization, and scaling in large learning systems has become a central problem at the interface of applied mathematics and machine learning theory.

This workshop is devoted to advancing the mathematical foundations of modern machine learning, with particular emphasis on connecting rigorous analysis to phenomena observed in large-scale practice. It will bring together researchers working on approximation theory, high-dimensional generalization, optimization dynamics, statistical physics perspectives, and the theory of large language models to clarify fundamental mechanisms underlying modern learning systems.

The planned session topics include:

  • Mathematical analysis of deep learning: expressivity, generalization, and optimization
  • Statistical physics approaches to training dynamics
  • Mathematical foundations of large-scale models and LLMs
